Compression ratios

Raw bitrate = width × height × color depth × fps. Color depth is 16 bits per pixel below 4K and 30 bits at 4K and above. Each codec divides the raw bitrate by its ratio; scene, quality and night IR then apply as usual.

    Generic presets are vendor-neutral planning values. UniFi presets use Ubiquiti's published resolution, maximum frame rate, IR and maximum PoE draw, and assume Enhanced (H.265) encoding; UniFi Protect sets each camera's bitrate from its quality level, so for a final design read the bitrate from the camera's settings and enter it as Measured. A datasheet or a measured stream always beats a preset.
    Cloud (VSaaS) groups are sized in GB for the plan and checked against the internet upload; SD-card groups are checked per camera against the card size. Neither uses the recorders. The archive tier sizes the recorders for the days kept on site and the archive for the rest.
    Raw-pixel method: width × height × color depth (16-bit below 4K, 30-bit at 4K and above) × fps ÷ the codec's compression ratio. It usually gives higher figures than typical bitrates, most of all at 4K. Use it to compare with tools that work this way; the SOP sizes with typical or measured bitrates.
    Estimates start from SOP Table 1 H.264 bitrates at 15 fps, medium motion and medium quality, then scale by codec (Smart H.264+ ×0.6, H.265 ×0.5, AV1 ×0.35, Smart H.265+ ×0.25, MJPEG ×5), frame rate, scene (×0.7 low, ×1.5 high), quality (×0.7 low, ×1.4 high) and night IR (+20%). Enter a measured bitrate to override the estimate for a group.
    With a Night FPS set, storage uses the day and night bitrates averaged over the night hours; throughput uses the higher of the two.
    Analog cameras (CIF, D1, 960H) connect to the DVR by coax, so switch and PoE do not apply to them. HD-over-coax cameras (HD-TVI, AHD, HD-CVI) are the same: pick their MP resolution and leave the switch as None.
    Throughput uses each camera's full bitrate, since every motion camera can trigger at once. Disk write is camera traffic ÷ 8. PoE estimates are 6 W per camera, plus 3 W with night IR and 2 W at 8 MP and above; enter the datasheet figure to override.
    Decimal units throughout: 1 TB = 1,000 GB. Disks are sold in TB; operating systems show TiB (1 TB = 0.909 TiB). Motion duty cycles include pre- and post-event recording.
